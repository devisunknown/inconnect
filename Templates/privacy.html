1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide when creating an account, such as your username, profile details, and the content you share. We may also collect usage data to improve the service.

We use your information to provide and personalize InConnect, connect you with other users, send notifications, and ensure the security of the platform.

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You may delete your account at any time.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the InConnect platform.
